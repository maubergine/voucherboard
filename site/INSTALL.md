# Install Voucherboard

Voucherboard comes as a browser extension and as an app for iPhone, iPad and Android. Each one works with Lewisham's
visitor parking permit site, `parkingpermits.lewisham.gov.uk`, in your own signed-in session.

## Browser extension

The extension works the same in every browser. The [guide's Install section](../extension/guide/README.md#install)
has the steps for each one, and the rest of the [guide](../extension/guide/README.md) shows how to use it.

## iPhone and iPad

Coming soon on the App Store. The app works with the council's site for you, so you don't need a browser extension.
Open it, sign in to your council account, and accept the terms.

### Testing on iPhone and iPad

Until the app is on the App Store, you can try it through Apple's TestFlight. Places are limited, so not everyone who
asks can join.

1. Email [voucherboard@mariusrubin.com](mailto:voucherboard@mariusrubin.com) with the email address of your Apple
   Account, and ask to be a tester.
2. Wait for an email from App Store Connect inviting you to join the Voucherboard team. Accept it and sign up with
   that Apple Account.
3. Wait for a second email, this time from TestFlight, inviting you to test Voucherboard. It only comes after you've
   joined the team.
4. Install [TestFlight](https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664) from the App Store, then open the
   TestFlight email on your iPhone or iPad and tap **View in TestFlight**. Accept the invitation and tap **Install**.

If TestFlight asks, turn on automatic updates, so you always have the latest build. You can also turn them on later on
the app's page in TestFlight. Each build stops working 90 days after it's released, so if you don't turn them on, install
new builds from TestFlight yourself, or the app will stop opening.
