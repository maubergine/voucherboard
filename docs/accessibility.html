Voucherboard accessibility statement

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This statement covers the Voucherboard app for iPhone. It doesn't yet cover the browser extension.

Voucherboard is made by Marius Rubin, an individual developer. It isn't made or endorsed by Lewisham Council or its suppliers. We want as many people as possible to be able to use it.

What you can do

You should be able to:

  • make the text up to the largest size iOS allows, with screens rearranging to fit
  • use VoiceOver, which reads out messages, errors and progress as they appear
  • use dark mode, or turn on Increase Contrast for stronger colours
  • turn on Reduce Motion
  • keep messages on screen for longer, or until you close them (More > Accessibility)
  • use the app with your phone turned sideways

Known limitations

  • The board: very short bookings are small to tap on the board's week and 4-week views. Open them from Bookings or Calendar instead.
  • Scanning a number plate needs the camera. You can always type the plate instead.
  • The council's own pages: signing in and buying vouchers happen on the council's website, shown inside the app. Those pages are run by the council's supplier, and this statement doesn't cover them.
  • Testing: the app hasn't yet been tested by hand with VoiceOver, Voice Control or Switch Control, or by disabled people. There may be problems we don't know about.

Feedback and reporting problems

If you find a problem not listed here, or you need something in a different format:

  • open an issue at https://github.com/maubergine/voucherboard/issues
  • email voucherboard@mariusrubin.com

Say what you were trying to do, which screen you were on, and which settings or assistive technology you use. Don't include your council sign-in details.

Technical information

Voucherboard isn't a public sector body, so the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018 don't apply to it. We use the standard those regulations use: the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2 at level AA.

Compliance status

Voucherboard for iPhone is partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 AA. A review in October 2026 found problems with large text, colour contrast, VoiceOver and how long messages stay on screen, and they have been fixed. The app hasn't yet been tested by hand with assistive technology, so we can't yet say it fully meets the standard.

Content that isn't within scope

The council's own pages, shown inside the app for signing in and buying vouchers, aren't made by Voucherboard.

How we tested

This statement was prepared on 12 October 2026. The developer reviewed the app's screens against WCAG 2.2 AA, checked colour contrast by calculation, and took Simulator screenshots at the largest text sizes, in dark mode, with Increase Contrast and in landscape. We'll update this statement after testing by hand.
